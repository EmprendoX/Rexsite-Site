Your logo, colors and contact detailsMenu: Settings

  1. 1Your logo: drag it into the box, or click to choose it from your computer (PNG, SVG, JPG, WEBP or GIF, up to 10 MB). No logo? Your website shows your business name instead.
  2. 2Your colors: click the color square to pick your main color (Primary) and your second color (Secondary).
A bit further down is Hero Image: the big photo on your homepage. Use your best photo.

Further down, under Broker Details, enter your contact details. These are the ones your clients see on your website.

  1. 3Broker Name is your name. Also fill in Phone, Email, City, Address and Slogan (a short line about you).
  2. 4Mobile number for text messages: digits only, starting with 1 and your area code, no + or spaces. Example: 13055551234. Your website's "Text us" button sends messages to this number.
  1. 5At the very bottom, click Save Settings. Your changes show on your website within a few minutes.

Social Media is optional: paste your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or LinkedIn link and it will appear on your website.

Your "About" pageMenu: About

This is where you tell people who you are. It's what a client reads before trusting you with their home.

  1. 1First, click English at the top, next to Language. Your website is in English, and each language is saved separately.
  2. 2Your photo, or your office's (Agency photo): click the box to upload it.

8

Add your first listingMenu: Properties

  1. 1Click + Create Property. This list is also where you edit or delete the listings you already have.

Fill out the form from top to bottom:

  1. 2ID: a number you haven't used yet (1 for your first listing, 2 for the second…). The Slug (the listing's web address) fills in by itself from the title.
  2. 3Title and Description. For example: "Modern family home with pool in Coral Gables".
  1. 4Type: For sale or For rent.
  2. 5Price: digits only, in US dollars. For rentals, it's the monthly rent.
  3. 6Address/Neighborhood and City.
  4. 7Features: choose the category (house, apartment, land…) and fill in Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Parking and Area (sq ft, required). Check Featured property to show it on your homepage.
  1. 8Photos: click Choose File and upload your photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF, up to 10 MB each). Without at least one photo, the listing isn't published.
  2. 9Click Create Property. It shows up on your website within a few minutes.

Your AI assistant (chat)Answers your visitors 24/7

A chat bubble 1 appears on your website. Your assistant answers using your published listings and, when someone wants to be contacted, sends you their details by email and in Leads.

Don't see Assistant and Conversations in your menu? Your AI assistant isn't active yet. It's an optional add-on: add Rexsite AI at www.rexsite.ai/start/plan, and we'll let you know as soon as it's active.

To set it up, open the Assistant menu:

  1. 2"Assistant turned on for my website": this box turns it on or off.
  2. 3Give it a name (for example, Maya) and choose the tone: Friendly or Formal.
  3. 4"About your business": tell it what you do, which areas you cover and what you offer.
  1. 5Frequently asked questions: write the questions you get most often, with their answers. For example: "Do you help with relocation?"
  2. 6Fill in "What it must not say" and your Business hours, then click "Save".
  3. 7With "Test my assistant" you can chat with it yourself, without using up conversations.
Important: your assistant uses what you write here to answer, and a visitor could end up seeing it. Don't include commissions, owners' details or anything private.

In the Conversations menu you can read everything your assistant talked about with your visitors in the last 30 days.

  1. 1Click a conversation in the list. The ones marked "Left their details" are also in Leads.
  2. 2On the right, you see the whole conversation.
11

Connect your AIMenu: Connect my AI · with Claude or ChatGPT

Connect your own AI to your website. From its chat you can search, create and edit your listings, and see who contacted you, just by chatting.

  1. 1Click "Create my private link".
  2. 2Click "Copy". Copy it right away: for security, it won't be shown again. Don't share it with anyone.
  3. 3Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT (instructions below).
Lost your link? Use "Create a new link".

In Claude

  1. Open Settings → Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Give it a name (for example, "My website") and paste your private link.
  4. Leave authentication blank and save it.
  5. In a new chat, turn on the connector and ask for what you need.

In ChatGPT

  1. Open Settings → Security and login and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Create a new app or connector with your private link, without authentication.
  3. In a new chat, pick that tool and ask for what you need.
ChatGPT's menu names may change.

What you can ask

  • "What listings do I have for rent?"
  • "Lower the price of the Coral Gables home to $2.3 million."
  • "Add a 2-bedroom condo for sale in Brickell: 1,100 sq ft, $650,000, with this photo: https://…"
  • "Who contacted me this week?"
Good to know: your AI asks for your OK before it creates or changes anything. To upload photos from your computer or phone, use Properties. You can't delete listings from your AI. Each change takes 1 or 2 minutes to show on your website.

These same instructions are in your panel, under "Connect my AI".
